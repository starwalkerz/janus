# Uganda — trend calendar 2027Q1–2038Q4
#
# Thesis: Uganda is the EAC's oil frontier. First commercial oil was delayed to
# June 2027, but the trajectory is intact: output rises to 145,000 b/d in 2027
# and peaks at 219,000–230,000 b/d by 2029. IMF estimates oil revenues at ~2%
# of GDP per year over the next decade. S&P cut the outlook to stable on oil
# delays and a widening fiscal deficit — growth decelerates to 5.8% in FY2027
# before the oil ramp. Debt-to-GDP is 52%, above the EAC convergence ceiling.
# The thesis is a delayed but powerful oil-cycle trade.
#
# W1 (2027–2029): Oil first, ramp-up, revenue inflection.
# W2 (2030–2032): Peak production; revenue consolidation.
# W3 (2033–2035): Oil revenue maturity; diversification begins.
# W4 (2036–2038): Post-oil-peak equilibrium; fiscal consolidation.
