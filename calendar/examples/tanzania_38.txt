# Tanzania — trend calendar 2027Q1–2038Q4
#

# Lindi LNG project FID has slipped into 2026-2027, with first cargo targeted

# 2032 and USD 3 billion by 2035 (~3% of 2024 GDP). Growth is 6.0-6.5% in
# 2026-27. The TAZARA rehabilitation and port infrastructure are the corridor

# W3 (2033–2035): LNG revenue peak; corridor maturity.
