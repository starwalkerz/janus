# Brazil — trend calendar 2027Q1–2038Q4
#
# Thesis: Brazil is the regional anchor with a commodity tailwind and a
# reindustrialization push, but a binding fiscal constraint. Growth is
# structurally modest — ~2.0–2.5% through 2030 — because the primary surplus
# path (0.5% of GDP in 2027 rising to 1.5% by 2030) is politically contested
# and debt is on an upward trajectory (86.6% of GDP by end-2026). The upside
# is not a China-style supercycle but a dual engine: oil production +80% by
# 2029 (potentially 3mn b/d by 2034) and an agricultural super-cycle with
# grain output +25%. Nova Indústria Brasil (NIB) and ENCTI 2024–2034 are the
# reindustrialization/IP transmission channels, but they are slow-moving
# relative to the commodity cycle.
#
# W1 (2027–2029): Commodity ramp; fiscal contestation; NIB early execution.
# W2 (2030–2032): Oil/agri peak contribution; fiscal targets bind.
# W3 (2033–2035): Commodity maturity; reindustrialization dividend visible.
# W4 (2036–2038): Post-commodity equilibrium; IP/tech carries the line.
