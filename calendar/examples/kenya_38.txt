# Kenya — trend calendar 2027Q1–2038Q4
#
# Thesis: Kenya is the EAC's logistics, services, and financial hub. Growth is
# steady but fiscally constrained — the binding constraint is the deficit and
# debt path, not demand. The IMF projects 4.7–5.3% growth through 2027-28, with
# the fiscal deficit declining from 5.9% of GDP in FY2027/28 to 5.4% in
# FY2028/29. Kenya is the "steady state" of the EAC — no oil shock, no LNG
# bonanza, but the deepest capital market and the most diversified economy.
#
# W1 (2027–2029): Fiscal consolidation; logistics corridor upgrades.
# W2 (2030–2032): Single-currency convergence; financial hub deepens.
# W3 (2033–2035): EAMU institutional build-out; services maturity.
# W4 (2036–2038): Stable-growth equilibrium; regional financial anchor.
