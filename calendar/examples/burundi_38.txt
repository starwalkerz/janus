# Burundi — trend calendar 2027Q1–2038Q4
#
# Thesis: Burundi is the EAC's frontier recovery state — the smallest, poorest,
# and most fragile. Growth averages 4.3% over 2027-2031, driven by coffee,
# gold, and electrification. Public debt is declining (42% of GDP in 2025 from
# 53% in 2024), but reserves cover only 1.6 months of imports and the exchange
# rate is overvalued. The 2027 elections are a near-term risk. This is a
# frontier-recovery trade, not a structural growth story.
#
# W1 (2027–2029): Stabilization; coffee/gold export recovery.
# W2 (2030–2032): Electrification; mining scale; debt decline.
# W3 (2033–2035): Frontier recovery matures; reserves rebuild.
# W4 (2036–2038): Stable frontier equilibrium; no structural unlock.
