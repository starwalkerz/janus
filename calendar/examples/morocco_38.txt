# Morocco — trend calendar 2027Q1–2038Q4
#
# Thesis: Morocco is a trade-oriented nearshoring NODE, not an anchor. Its
# growth is EU-FDI-transmitted. The 2030 co-production milestone is the peak
# of the dependency, not an escape from it. The EU's "Made in Europe" push
# (20% manufacturing share by 2035; 80% car production within Europe) is the
# structural threat to the nearshoring rent. The calendar encodes a defined
# horizon: accumulate through the 2030 peak, dampen as EU industrial policy
# bites in the mid-2030s.
#
# W1 (2027–2029): Nearshoring deepens; aerospace/EV ramp.
# W2 (2030–2032): 2030 peak — 2M vehicles, FIFA World Cup, co-production hub.
# W3 (2033–2035): "Made in Europe" challenge peaks; nearshoring rent erodes.
# W4 (2036–2038): Consolidation as a compliant nearshore extension, not a
#   low-cost base.
