# Great youth culture & pop culture phase change — 2027Q1–2038Q4
# Atlantic → Indian Ocean rotation
#
# Thesis: pop culture production and consumption rotate from the Atlantic
# (us, uk, ca) to the Indian Ocean (in, sg, hk) across the window.
# Sectors: consumer (fashion), tech (streaming, gaming, social).
#
# W1 (2027–2029): ATLANTIC PREDOMINANCE — Atlantic consumer + tech favor.
#   Indian Ocean tech emerging (in streaming, sg content hub) but not yet
#   consumer (Indian fashion not yet global).
# W2 (2030–2032): BOTH FAVOR — Atlantic still leads, Indian Ocean tech
#   escalates.
# W3 (2033–2035): ATLANTIC CONSUMER SATURATION — Atlantic fashion cycles
#   tire; aging demographics erode trend adoption. Indian Ocean consumer
#   begins to favor (Indian fashion goes global).
# W4 (2036–2038): ATLANTIC CONTENT SATURATION — Atlantic streaming and
#   gaming saturate; Indian Ocean consumer + tech both favor.
