# Ethiopia + EAC — trend calendar 2027Q1–2038Q4
#
# Thesis: Ethiopia is the only structural (non-cyclic) line in the swing-state
# set. EAC accession is ruled out near-term (AfCFTA is the immediate priority),
# but functional integration deepens through capital markets, trade corridors,
# and legal harmonization. The ESX listing pipeline is the transmission channel:
# 50 IPOs targeted by 2030, 90 within a decade. Berbera is the near-term port
# hedge; Lamu/LAPSSET is the long-term deep-water alternative. Regional
# conflicts are a persistent valuation discount (priced), not a continuity
# break — no dampen override is applied.
#
# W1 (2027–2029): AfCFTA operationalization; ESX ramp; Berbera access.
# W2 (2030–2032): EAC candidacy advances; ESX 50-IPO milestone; Lamu corridor.
# W3 (2033–2035): EAC convergence; private-sector-led growth consolidates.
# W4 (2036–2038): 2036 top-economy goal; structural hold continues.
