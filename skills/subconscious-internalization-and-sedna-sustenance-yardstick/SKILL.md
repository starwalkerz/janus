#### Subconscious Internalization & Sedna-Sustenance Yardstick

You are reading the tick against the **structural internalization and
deep recurring-sustenance cycle**—the direct mirror to
vernal-synchronization-and-pan-ignition-yardstick (Pan / *A
Midsummer Night's Dream*). 

Where **Pan** represents the *visible, external vernal wave*—the
wizard's tune that ignites collective attention at the cusp of spring and
dissipates by Midsummer—**Sedna** (from the foundational Inuit oral
cosmology; Franz Boas, *The Central Eskimo*, 1888; Knud Rasmussen,
*Intellectual Culture of the Iglulik Eskimos*, 1929) models **what
happens after the external enchantment is severed, sinks beneath the
surface into the deep collective subconscious, and returns continuously
as the daily sustenance that keeps the village alive**: 

1. **The Enchantment & Severance (External Shock Meets Institutional Fear):**
Sedna follows the seductive tune of the wizard (Pan’s external
catalyst), finds the raw force overwhelming, and turns back toward
her father's boat. Frightened by the upheaval, the father severs her
fingers at the gunwale, and she sinks into the deep ocean—analogous
to the post-hype shakeout, regulatory backlash, or structural
submersion after an external boom subsides.
2. **Deep Submersion & Collective Internalization (Carl Jung + Thomas Schelling + Path-Dependent Hysteresis):**
Beneath the visible surface (subconscious / non-headline operational depth), the severed fragments transform into **all living creatures
of the sea**—silent, enduring, foundational infrastructure embedded
in everyday habit rather than loud media chatter (Carl G. Jung, *The
Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious*, 1959; Thomas C.
Schelling, *The Strategy of Conflict*, 1960; Paul A. David, *"Clio
and the Economics of QWERTY,"* 1985).
3. **The Return Through Daily Sustenance (Recurring Real Yield):**
Because the village lives by harvesting from the sea, Sedna returns
home continuously—not as a noisy external spectacle, but as **steady,
indispensable recurring sustenance (cash flow, habitual adoption, and compounding real output)**. Conversely, when a polity or firm
violates reciprocity and refuses to tend the deep source, Sedna
withholds the sea harvest, starving the village of real yield while
surface chatter remains empty.

Applying this mirror across any jurisdiction, company, technology, or
asset class separates four internalization regimes: 

* **Internalized Sea Sustenance (INTERNALIZED_SEA_SUSTENANCE):** The
initial external hype (Pan) has already submerged into silent,
habitual, everyday infrastructure (high subconscious internalization)
that delivers steady, recurring real yield (1.0 pass).
* **Submersion & Gestation (SUBMERSION_GESTATION_PHASE):** The
external wave has recently submerged past its peak (post-severance shakeout); foundational utility is forming beneath the surface
before full recurring yield matures (0.65 cap).
* **Surface-Only Spectacle (UNINTERNALIZED_SURFACE_FAD):** High
external noise (Pan’s tune) with zero deep behavioral or
cash-flow internalization—nothing sinks into lasting infrastructure
(0.25 cap).
* **Withheld Sustenance Famine (SEDNA_WITHHELD_SUBSISTENCE_FAMINE):**
Both surface vitality and deep structural yield are starved—the
polity or enterprise has severed its generative source without
internalizing real compounding (0.10 cap, halving to 0.05 under
persistent subsistence drought).
