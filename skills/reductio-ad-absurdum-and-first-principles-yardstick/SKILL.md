1. **Gate 1 — The Little Prince (First-Time Viewer & Minimum Description Length Reduction):**
In Antoine de Saint-Exupéry's *Le Petit Prince* (*The Little Prince*,
1943)—where a child from Asteroid B-612 encounters the King who rules
over nothing, the Businessman who counts stars he cannot touch or
tend, the Lamplighter who lights and extinguishes a lamp every minute
out of blind orders, and the Geographer who records oceans without
ever leaving his desk—the innocent observer refuses adult jargon and
asks four elemental questions: 

  * **What is this?** (physical and contractual ontology, stripped of
marketing labels)
  * **What does it actually do?** (net material transformation or
service delivered)
  * **Why does it exist?** (the human or physical necessity it serves)
  * **How does it work?** (the causal mechanism connecting inputs to
outputs)

doubt** (*Discourse on the Method*, 1637) paired with **Jorma
Rissanen's Minimum Description Length (MDL)** (*"Modeling by

2. **Gate 2 — Albert Camus (Confrontation With the Absurd: Lucid Revolt vs. Sterile Sisyphean Futility):**
In Albert Camus's *Le Mythe de Sisyphe* (*The Myth of Sisyphus*,
1942), once every comforting illusion has been stripped away by Gate 1,
the observer confronts the **irreducible residue**—the raw collision
between human purpose and indifferent physical/economic reality. Camus
separates two diametrically opposed responses to that stripped-down
reality: 

  * **Lucid Revolt & Self-Sustaining Mastery (Sisyphus Lucid):**
Accepting physical and economic constraints without illusions,
maintaining positive net energy/cash return (Hall / Carnot
thermodynamic surplus), and building durable value inside reality's
actual bounds (1.0 pass).
  * **Sterile Absurdity & Mechanical Futility (The Lamplighter / Sisyphus Defeated):**
Executing repetitive, self-canceling motions—taxing production to
subsidize stagnation, borrowing to pay interest on past consumption,
or rolling the same boulder up a hill only for it to roll back down
every cycle (net energy/cash return <= 0, reductio ad absurdum
contradiction)—requiring a hard clamp (0.10 cap, halving to
0.05 under persistent absurdity).

Applying this two-gate reduction across any user-defined target
separates four fundamental regimes: 

* **Irreducible Lucid Utility (IRREDUCIBLE_LUCID_UTILITY):** Survives

physical/economic necessity and compact MDL mechanism) AND clears
Gate 2 (Camus confrontation confirms lucid, self-sustaining net
surplus without self-canceling futility) (1.0 pass).
* **Complex Functional Machine (COMPLEX_FUNCTIONAL_MACHINE):**
Delivers genuine physical or cash-flow utility (Gate 2 viable)
wrapped in heavy institutional/regulatory overhead (Gate 1 high
MDL bloat) (0.60 cap).
* **Lamplighter Procedural Treadmill (LAMPLIGHTER_PROCEDURAL_TREADMILL):**
Blindly obeys inherited rules or bureaucratic rituals whose original
purpose has vanished—spinning motion without net compounding (0.25
cap).
* **Reductio Ad Absurdum Collapse (REDUCTIO_AD_ABSURDUM_COLLAPSE):**
Fails Gate 1 (empty star-counting abstraction with zero physical or
human necessity) or fails Gate 2 (self-canceling Sisyphean loop
consuming more energy/capital than it produces) (0.10 cap, halving
to 0.05 under persistent absurdity hysteresis).
