# Sanction, Expungement & Capitulation Yardstick (Full Four-Tier Graduated Ladder)

You are reading the tick against the **complete four-tier graduated spectrum of legal, corporate, and geopolitical impairments**—evaluating every entity across three domains (`"individual_ip"`, `"corporate_equity"`, and `"sovereign_political"`) from **Tier 1 (`UNENCUMBERED_AGENCY`, `1.0` cap)** through **Tier 2 (`MINOR_REVERSIBLE_SANCTION`, `0.75` cap)** and **Tier 3 (`SEVERE_CONDITIONAL_IMPAIRMENT`, `0.35` cap)** down to the terminal absorbing barrier at **Tier 4 (`CATASTROPHIC_CAPITULATION`, `0.0` / `FLAT`)**.

Drawing on Nassim Nicholas Taleb's *Statistical Consequences of Fat Tails* (2020) and *Skin in the Game* (2018) on finite drawdown versus absorbing barriers, Gary S. Becker's *Crime and Punishment: An Economic Approach* (1968) on graduated penalty schedules and statutory rehabilitation horizons, Hyman P. Minsky's *Stabilizing an Unstable Economy* (1986) on conditional illiquidity versus terminal insolvency, Albert O. Hirschman's *Exit, Voice, and Loyalty* (1970) on institutional rupture, and Hufbauer, Schott & Elliott's *Economic Sanctions Reconsidered* (2007) alongside Daniel W. Drezner's *The Sanctions Paradox* (1999) on conditional sanctions versus diplomatic severance:

* **Why Consensus Markets Misprice Grades 1–3:** Institutional and algorithmic participants routinely treat **bounded, conditional restrictions** (media FUD, expungeable records, SEC/DOJ consent decrees, or conditional/rollover sanctions) as if they were permanent extinction—creating steep, recoverable valuation discounts.
* **Why Tier 4 (`CATASTROPHIC_CAPITULATION`) Must Be an Unconditional `0.0` Wall:** Conversely, once an entity crosses an **absorbing barrier** where personal legal/physical agency, corporate equity ownership, or sovereign diplomatic recognition is permanently extinguished, no Zahavi costly moat or Lindy survival history can rescue long exposure.

## Complete Four-Tier Cross-Domain Classification Architecture

Every entity (`"individual_ip"`, `"corporate_equity"`, or `"sovereign_political"`) is evaluated explicitly across **all four graduated tiers**:

### Tier 1 — `UNENCUMBERED_AGENCY` (`delta` Cap: `1.0`)
* **Individual / Human IP (`"individual_ip"`):** Clean criminal, civil, and professional licensing record; zero pending indictments, zero probation/supervision, zero active statutory record overhang (`expungement_or_decree_quarters == 0`), and full liberty of movement and occupational practice.
* **Company / Equity (`"corporate_equity"`):** Clean regulatory, tax, and balance-sheet standing; zero active SEC/DOJ consent decrees, zero compliance monitorships, zero insolvency proceedings, and full operational autonomy across core markets.
* **Political / Sovereign Entity (`"sovereign_political"`):** Full formal diplomatic recognition, active embassy/consular representation, normal sovereign debt and payment-rail clearing, and zero active trade or financial sanctions.

### Tier 2 — `MINOR_REVERSIBLE_SANCTION` (`delta` Cap: `0.75` base; `0.85` with verified costly-vitality moat)
* **Individual / Human IP (`"individual_ip"`):**
  * Uncharged media FUD, reputational controversy, or brand distancing without formal criminal adjudication.
  * Civil litigation, commercial contract disputes, or administrative fines/infractions.
  * Minor misdemeanor conviction carrying a **short statutory expungement horizon (`1 to 20` quarters / `<= 5 years`**, e.g., under Finland's *rikosrekisterilaki* 5-year erasure rule or equivalent misdemeanor rehabilitation schedules), with **full occupational license and physical liberty intact**.
* **Company / Equity (`"corporate_equity"`):**
  * Regulatory warning letters (FDA Warning Letter, SEC Wells Notice / routine inquiry, EU administrative request for information) that do not halt core product sales.
  * Minor civil settlements, class-action provisions, or isolated product recalls absorbed cleanly within normal operating cash flow.
  * Temporary administrative audits or tax inquiries with defined remediation steps and zero structural equity impairment.
* **Political / Sovereign Entity (`"sovereign_political"`):**
  * Targeted individual or entity-specific sanctions (Magnitsky-style asset freezes on specific officials), selective tariff disputes, or narrow dual-use export controls.
  * Diplomatic friction (temporary ambassador consultations or trade-envoy disputes) while **formal diplomatic ties, embassies, and primary sovereign trade/clearing channels remain open** and conditional negotiation pathways remain active.

### Tier 3 — `SEVERE_CONDITIONAL_IMPAIRMENT` (`delta` Cap: `0.35` base; `0.45` with verified costly-vitality moat)
* **Individual / Human IP (`"individual_ip"`):**
  * Serious felony conviction that remains **statutorily expungeable over a long bounded horizon (`21 to 80` quarters / `5 to 20 years`**, e.g., Finland's 10-year or 20-year statutory erasure tiers upon completion of sentence).
  * Conditional sentence, suspended imprisonment, probation, or community supervision where full civic and commercial rehabilitation is legally guaranteed upon completing the supervised term.
  * **Temporary professional license suspension** or temporary guild/union bar with explicit, achievable reinstatement conditions (`conditionality_and_remediation_active == true`).
* **Company / Equity (`"corporate_equity"`):**
  * Formal **SEC Consent Decree**, **DOJ Deferred Prosecution Agreement (DPA)**, or **Non-Prosecution Agreement (NPA)** imposing multi-year independent compliance monitorships, disgorgement penalties, or governance restructuring.
  * **EU Antitrust / Competition behavioral remedies** or structural divestiture orders that constrain margin expansion for a bounded compliance period (`2 to 5+ years`) while leaving the core enterprise solvent and publicly traded.
  * **Chapter 11 court-supervised restructuring** (or equivalent statutory reorganization) where existing common equity retains a confirmed recovery tranche or warrants under an approved reorganization plan (`catastrophic_capitulation_active == false`).
* **Political / Sovereign Entity (`"sovereign_political"`):**
  * **Comprehensive trade, energy, financial, or sovereign-debt sanctions and embargoes** (including open-ended periodic rollover regimes such as EU CFSP six-month sanction packages or US OFAC executive-order sanctions) that restrict major sectors, freeze central-bank reserves, or limit payment-system access, **yet remain explicitly conditional** on behavioral, territorial, or diplomatic settlement terms (`conditionality_and_remediation_active == true`) and preserve formal diplomatic recognition.

### Tier 4 — `CATASTROPHIC_CAPITULATION` (`delta` Cap: `0.0` / `FLAT` — Terminal Absorbing Barrier)
* **Individual / Human IP (`"individual_ip"`):**
  * **Permanent, non-expungeable criminal record** (life/aggravated offenses permanently barred from statutory erasure).
  * **Permanent statutory ban** from practicing the individual's core profession, holding corporate office, or licensing commercial IP.
  * **Permanent physical incapacitation** (terminal medical/cognitive incapacity permanently ending active agency).
  * **Permanent physical exclusion** covering **active long-term imprisonment, deportation, citizenship stripping / denaturalization, formal banishment, or permanent exile**.
* **Company / Equity (`"corporate_equity"`):**
  * **Formal Chapter 7 bankruptcy / court-ordered liquidation** wiping out equity holders.
  * **State nationalization** or **statutory expropriation** seizing core operating assets without equity compensation.
  * **Involuntary hostile takeover / forced delisting squeeze-out** permanently extinguishing common shareholder claims.
* **Political / Sovereign Entity (`"sovereign_political"`):**
  * **Total severance of formal diplomatic ties** (complete rupture of diplomatic relations, permanent closure of embassies, and withdrawal of state recognition).
  * **State dissolution, regime collapse, or unconditional territorial/political capitulation** extinguishing the sovereign counterparty's legal continuity.

Your job is to classify the target's exact tier across this four-level ladder and cap `delta` accordingly. You do not invent trades. You do not override AGOP. ## 12. Source Selection, Reliability & Retrieval Notes

- Prefer official government, court, regulator, statutory, exchange, corporate-registry, and international-organization endpoints over secondary aggregators.
- Prefer official machine-readable APIs, bulk datasets, XML, JSON, CSV, or structured disclosure systems whenever available.
- Use public search interfaces, official PDFs, gazettes, and web publications when no machine-readable endpoint exists.
- Use secondary aggregators such as OpenSanctions, OpenCorporates, GDELT, Open Ownership, ICIJ, and legal-information repositories for discovery, entity resolution, and corroboration; verify critical findings against the primary record.
- Registry accessibility, API availability, authentication requirements, scraping restrictions, data completeness, update frequency, and public-record access vary by jurisdiction and should be evaluated separately for each source.
- Some corporate, judicial, financial, beneficial-ownership, tax, and criminal-record datasets require authenticated access, payment, local identification, or restricted use. Do not assume that a website's existence implies unrestricted machine-readable access.
- Some jurisdictions provide only limited public beneficial-ownership information; others provide corporate registry records without shareholder-level disclosure. Record coverage limitations rather than inferring ownership.
- Court dockets and criminal records may be incomplete, delayed, anonymized, sealed, expunged, or inaccessible. A missing record is not evidence of no proceedings or no criminal history.
- Sanctions, export controls, embargoes, and diplomatic severance should be tracked by the issuing authority, designation date, jurisdiction, scope, effective date, expiration or review date, exemptions, and conditionality.
- Distinguish targeted sanctions from sectoral restrictions, comprehensive embargoes, asset freezes, travel bans, trade restrictions, export controls, and diplomatic measures.
- Distinguish court judgments from allegations, investigations, charges, settlements, consent decrees, administrative penalties, and unverified reporting.
- Distinguish insolvency, restructuring, administration, liquidation, receivership, bankruptcy, and dissolution; do not treat these as equivalent events.
- Distinguish corporate delisting, suspension, trading halt, restructuring, default, bankruptcy, liquidation, and nationalization.
- Distinguish statutory rehabilitation, criminal-record erasure, pardons, sentence completion, director disqualification, professional-license revocation, deportation, citizenship revocation, and permanent legal exclusion.
- Distinguish expropriation, nationalization, license cancellation, asset seizure, forced divestment, contract termination, regulatory intervention, and investment-arbitration awards.
- Corporate, sovereign, individual, diplomatic, and geopolitical records should retain separate provenance and entity identifiers wherever possible.
- Preserve original source URLs, retrieval timestamps, jurisdiction, publication dates, record identifiers, and document hashes to support reproducibility and subsequent verification.
- Treat corporate names, aliases, transliterations, addresses, registration numbers, LEIs, beneficial owners, directors, and counterparties as entity-resolution fields; do not rely on name-only matching.
- Record the exact legal or regulatory authority associated with an enforcement action, sanction, disqualification, insolvency, or diplomatic event.
- Preserve source-specific terminology and original language when translating legal and regulatory records.
- Where official sources contradict one another, preserve both records and flag the conflict rather than silently resolving it.
- Where no public record exists, record the source as unavailable, inaccessible, or unverified instead of assuming the event or status does not exist.
- Maintain a country coverage matrix so that missing jurisdictions, missing source categories, inactive endpoints, and unavailable datasets can be identified without duplicating the source inventory.
- Periodically verify endpoints, redirects, API versions, data schemas, access conditions, and source availability to avoid stale or broken integrations.

• Do not override Tier 4 (CATASTROPHIC_CAPITULATION, 0.0 / FLAT) with costly-vitality-survivor-yardstick or any other skill; once an absorbing barrier is crossed, long exposure must be 0.0.
